# Final Year Project Synopsis Template

Replace every line in [square brackets]. A synopsis is usually three to six pages; it gets
your topic approved, so every section must be specific enough that a guide can say yes to it.
The structure follows https://campus.streamerosai.com/guides/final-year-project-synopsis-format/.
If your college issues its own synopsis format, follow that and use this for the content.
